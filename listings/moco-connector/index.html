Streamline your HR processes with MOCO and Camunda

Automate HR processes and synchronize data effortlessly with the MORO ERP Connector for Camunda. Boost productivity and streamline workflows with seamless integration into your existing systems.

Features

User and Team Management

Manage user and team entities and integrate them into your on- and off-boarding processes without disrupting your current workflows.

Manage Absences for Accurate Planning

Align your schedule with planned and unplanned absences to be always up to date in your planning.

Get Absence Reporting for Better Oversight

Integrate absence numbers into your planning processes. Easily track and analyze patterns without the need for additional tools.

Travel Expense Integration

Create your own customized travel expense process and integrate MOCO right into it as the leading data system.

Invoice Handling

Integrate invoice handling into your HR and financial processes, allowing you e.g. to monitor and manage late payments.
