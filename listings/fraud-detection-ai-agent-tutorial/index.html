Detect and manage insurance fraud with agentic AI

This blueprint shows how you can use the BPMN ad-hoc sub-process to blend deterministic and non-deterministic execution, leveraging AI agents for case management use cases.

Features

Customers receive instant feedback

The AI agent drafts and sends an email autonomously, dramatically shortening resolution time for fraud handling.

Blend AI and human intervention

De-risk agentic AI use cases by having AI perform tedious, time-sensitive work under the supervision of a human expert. You can even have humans review and approve customer-facing actions.

Improve operating efficiency

Provide a superior customer experience while dramatically decreasing labor costs by blending the right level of AI for your organization.
