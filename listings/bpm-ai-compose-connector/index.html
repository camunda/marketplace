Compose text for emails and more using AI

Automatically generate text for emails, chat messages, or social media posts using Large Language Models (LLMs).

Features

Dynamic Content Composition

Easily automate the creation of personalized emails, social media messages, and other forms of written communication. This Connector uses input variables and customizable templates to dynamically generate content that feels personal and engaging.

Template-Driven Generation

By defining text templates with variable placeholders and generation instructions, you can ensure a consistent structure of the resulting text and incorporate process variables without exposing them to the LLM.

Tone and Style Settings

Maintain the right tone and style across all your communication with adjustable settings. Whether you need a formal tone for business emails or a friendly approach for social media interactions, this Connector adapts to the desired tone and style to fit the context of your message.

Multilingual Support

Break language barriers with the ability to compose messages in various languages.
