Lot conversion process orchestration for commercial to clinical case execution

Automate out-of-spec (OOS) lot conversion for cell therapy with agentic process orchestration, physician communication capture, and full auditability for compliance. Agentic orchestration handles real-time physician communications and routes actions through governed processes with human-in-the-loop oversight.

Features

Orchestrate cross-team lot conversion

Automates and orchestrate the end-to-end lot conversion process during an OOS event, ensuring faster and more reliable decision-making without compromising compliance. Reduce manual intervention, enable real-time data absorption, support AI-driven disposition decisions, and ensure synchronized updates across downstream enterprise systems.

Real-time process orchestration

Coordinates lot conversion processes from commercial operations to clinical execution, minimizing disruption to patient treatment timelines.

Agent-driven decision support

Automatically extracts data from physician emails and QC reports, improving accuracy and accelerating lot disposition decisions.

Human-in-the-loop governance

Maintains regulatory compliance and quality oversight while reducing manual workload and error risk.

Multi-system integration with downstream updates

Synchronizes updates across quality, manufacturing, regulatory, and supply chain systems in real time, eliminating reconciliation gaps.

Enhanced traceability and scalability

Provides end-to-end auditability and a scalable automation framework to support growing commercial cell therapy operations.
