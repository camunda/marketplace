Extend Your Camunda Processes with Blue Prism RPA

Unlock the full potential of RPA within your Camunda processes with the SS&C Blue Prism Connector. Seamlessly add work items to Blue Prism queues and monitor their status in real-time, enriching your business process orchestration capabilities.

Features

Add Work Item to the Queue

Automate the queuing of tasks in Blue Prism by triggering this action from within a Camunda process. This feature enables you to offload repetitive, rule-based tasks to Blue Prism bots, allowing for a more scalable and efficient workflow. The queuing can be conditional based on data points or events captured in Camunda.

Get Work Item Status

Keep track of the automated tasks you’ve queued in Blue Prism directly within your Camunda processes. This feature provides real-time visibility into the status of each work item, helping you make informed decisions and maintain smooth operations. You can use this data for logging, error handling, or triggering subsequent steps in a business process.
