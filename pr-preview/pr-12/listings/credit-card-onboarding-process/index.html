Credit Card Onboarding Process

Wipro Limited Partner

Contact

Quickly and efficiently onboard customers with minimal manual work, hassle-free digital KYC verification, and short duration for card application request completion.

Features

Simplified application process

Digital applications eliminate the need for extensive paperwork, saving time and resources for both applicants and financial institutions, reducing the potential for errors and delays.

Communication and notifications

The application process includes timely notifications to inform customers about card dispatch, rejections, or any other updates ensuring customers are aware of their application’s status and any actions they need to take.

Intelligent document extraction

Camunda IDP can extract the application form data uploaded by customers or staff harnessing AI models for smart data extraction.

Simplified workflow

The blueprint contains implementation using business rules, SLAs, automated task executions, and smooth KYC through Zoom meeting integration can also be done.
