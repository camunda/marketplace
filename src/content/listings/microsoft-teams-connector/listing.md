Bring Microsoft Teams into Camunda processes to facilitate real-time communication, notifications, and contextual workflow enhancements.

## Features

### Get Chat by ID

Retrieve specific chat data from Teams to inform or drive subsequent activities in Camunda processes, such as condition-based branching or notifications.

### List Chat Members

Enumerate members of a chat to establish context or permissions in orchestrated processes; for example, dynamic task assignment based on chat participants.

### Send Message in Chat

Enable Camunda to send messages in a Teams chat, be it for notifications, status updates, or data collection within a workflow.

### List Messages in Chat

Obtain a list of chat messages to process historical data, sentiment analysis, or compliance checks within Camunda processes.

### Get Message in Chat

Retrieve specific messages for fine-grained information extraction or context establishment in Camunda.

### Create Channel

Automatically create new Teams channels from Camunda for project-based collaborations or specialized communication streams.

### Get Channel

Fetch details of a specific Teams channel to facilitate information-driven or context-driven actions in Camunda.

### List Channels

Enumerate all available channels to aid in decision-making or routing within orchestrated Camunda processes.

### Send Message to Channel

Disseminate messages to Teams channels directly from Camunda. This feature is useful for announcements, alerts, and data sharing.

### Get Channel Message

Extract specific messages from a channel for targeted actions or data utilization in Camunda-based processes.

### List Channel Messages

Gather a list of all messages within a channel for aggregate processing, reporting, or auditing in Camunda.

### List Message Replies

Get replies to a specific message for threaded conversations, thereby enhancing context and data collection within workflows.

### List Members

List channel members to establish context or to perform role-based assignments or notifications within Camunda.
