Exodus by Capital BPM is a streamlined solution designed for fast, reliable, and cost-effective migration from Camunda 7 to Camunda 8.

## Features

### Migrate processes and decision tables

Evolve BPMN processes and DMN decision tables with automatic script migration and input/output mapping.

### Migrate delegates and listeners

Migrating delegates and listeners to corresponding Zeebe job workers that adhere to best practices and include unit tests.

### Guaranteed success

Achieve a 100% guaranteed success at a fraction of the time and cost of other migration strategies.
