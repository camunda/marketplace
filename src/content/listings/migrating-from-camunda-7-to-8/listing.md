envite consulting is a top-level Platinum Camunda Partner delivering reliable, future-proof migrations from Camunda 7 to Camunda 8.

## Features

### Scope & Migration Readiness

We assess your existing processes, integrations, and operational constraints to establish a clear migration baseline. Based on this, we define a realistic and transparent migration scope that aligns with your technical and organizational goals.

### Review & Planning

We review your current technology stack and target architecture to design a tailored migration plan. Architectural and conceptual differences between Camunda 7 and Camunda 8 are explicitly considered to avoid surprises during implementation.

### Application & Data Migration

We migrate process models and application code using a test-driven approach supported by automated migration tooling. Clean refactoring and solid test coverage ensure functional parity while enabling the adoption of Camunda 8 concepts where appropriate. Depending on your requirements, we support both drain-out and big-bang data migration strategies.

### Testing & Validation

We systematically test and validate migrated processes and applications to ensure correctness, performance, and operational stability in the Camunda 8 environment.
