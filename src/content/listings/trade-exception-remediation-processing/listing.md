Automatically identify errors and exceptions that require expert intervention. Involve knowledge workers only when necessary to facilitate pre-trade reconciliation. Prevent delays and cost overruns caused by manual reconciliation work.

## Features

### Business Validation

Automatically identifies errors and exceptions that require expert intervention.

### Humans in the Loop

Orchestrates human tasks to involve knowledge workers (analysts) only when necessary to facilitate pre-trade reconciliation.

### Cost Efficiency

Prevents delays and cost overruns caused by manual reconciliation work.
