Automate Zammad ticket management in Camunda 8. Create, retrieve, update, and delete support tickets, search users, and create customers directly from BPMN processes using the Zammad connector.

## Features

### Manage the complete ticket lifecycle

Manage the entire support ticket lifecycle directly from Camunda processes. Create, retrieve, update, and delete Zammad tickets to automate customer support, incident management, and service request processes.

### Automate customer support processes

Automate customer support processes by combining user lookup, customer creation, and ticket management within a single Camunda process, reducing manual effort and improving response times.

### Integrate seamlessly into your BPMN models

Use the connector directly in Camunda BPMN process models to orchestrate Zammad support operations as part of end-to-end business processes.
