Orchestrate and automate the car rental booking process, including issues such as a reserved car not being available or a rented car not being returned on time.

## Features

### Feature-Rich Blueprint

This blueprint demonstrates how to handle checks and validations, car availability and readiness, and an escalation process on late return.

### Easy to Define KPIs

This blueprint is a prime example of using various types of events to enable KPI reports in Camunda Optimize.

### Adaptable Subprocesses

The well-structured process can be easily adapted to fit your specific use case.
