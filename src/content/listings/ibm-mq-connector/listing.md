Allow Camunda-based business processes to send messages to and receive messages from IBM MQ queues as part of a service task, enabling asynchronous communication and decoupling of processes.

## Features

### Easy queue messaging

Connects Camunda-based processes directly to IBM MQ queue managers for send and receive operations.

### Standard-compliant

Leverages JMS-like APIs and IBM MQ client libraries for enterprise interoperability.

### Robust security

Implements TLS/SSL encryption, username/password authentication, and secure channel configurations.
