### Continuous process analysis

AI monitors execution data and evaluates performance indicators in real time.

Identifies delays, recurring incidents, and performance deviations.

### AI-generated recommendations

Produces actionable insights to improve business processes and resource allocation.

Triggers improvement workflows directly in Camunda.
