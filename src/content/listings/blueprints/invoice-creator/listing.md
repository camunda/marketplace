*Create and Review Invoices*

Create and review an invoice with a dynamic list of items and automatic total and tax calculations in a drag-and-drop editor.

## Features

### Automatic Calculations

The sample form illustrates how you can automatically calculate taxes and totals in a Camunda form.

### Drag-and-Drop Customization

Ensure the form meets your business requirements by customizing and extending it using Camunda's what-you-see-is-what-you-get (WYSIWYG) editor.
