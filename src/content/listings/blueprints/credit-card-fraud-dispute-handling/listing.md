Handling credit card fraud disputes can be expensive and time-consuming. This blueprint illustrates how to improve the process and increase customer satisfaction through a combination of automated and manual tasks.

## Features

### Time efficiency

Reduce average transaction time by 40% with end-to-end orchestration of the process between automated and manual tasks.

### Extend with AI capabilities

Reduce average wait time and average handling time by 60% through data pre-fill, enrichment, and decision support using artificial intelligence and machine learning.

### Extend with connectivity

Reduce customer call rates by 32% through multi-channel customer self-service.
