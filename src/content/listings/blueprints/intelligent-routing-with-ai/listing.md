*Optimize Workflows with AI Routing*

Use AI to intelligently route customer inquiries to the appropriate department for improved efficiency and resolution.

## Features

### Contextualising Signals

Process customer signals with the OpenAI Connector and business-context prompts.

### Task Intelligence

Leverage process and decisions to route requests to the appropriate department or systems.

### Approve or Reroute

Design loops with human feedback and ask OpenAI to improve its decisions.
