*Orchestrate Hardware and Mobile Orders in Parallel*

This blueprint addresses challenges around retail mobile device orders to ensure a positive customer experience and reduce the chance of fraud.

## Features

### Synchronous and Asynchronous Interfaces

This blueprint showcases synchronous and asynchronous communication with systems that are external to the process.

### Flexible and Parallel Fulfillments

Partial orders for hardware and mobile can be easily fulfilled in parallel via an inclusive gateway and multi-instance markers.

### Validate and Decompose with FEEL

The Friendly-Enough Expression Language (FEEL) is a powerful tool to validate and map relevant input data inside the BPMN process.
