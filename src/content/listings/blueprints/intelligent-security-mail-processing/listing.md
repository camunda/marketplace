Employees forward suspicious emails and receive an AI-generated Guardian Report with a clear verdict. Camunda orchestrates privacy-safe threat analysis with Amazon Bedrock.

## Features

### Report suspicious emails effortlessly

Forward a suspicious email to a dedicated mailbox, and the process takes over from there. You need no tools, training, or security knowledge.

### Protect privacy by design

Detect and mask names and other personal data before any AI analysis, and keep GDPR requirements in focus with a single EU-hosted data processor.

### Classify threats with AI

Classify threats such as phishing, CEO fraud, malware, scams, and ransomware with an LLM on Amazon Bedrock, and rate severity and confidence. Forwarding-aware logic reliably distinguishes the forwarder from the original sender.

### Get an actionable Guardian Report

Receive a clear HTML report with threat type, severity, and concrete recommended actions, and turn uncertainty into the right next step within minutes.

### Orchestrate the entire process with Camunda

Orchestrate intake, metadata retrieval, anonymization, AI assessment, and report delivery with Camunda, and gain full transparency and traceability for every analyzed email.
