*Optimized Decision Making with Azure OpenAI*

Operationalize AI by leveraging the power of Microsoft's suite of AI services with Camunda process orchestration.

## Features

### Contextualizing Signals

Process customer signals with the Azure OpenAI Connector and business-context prompts.

### Task Intelligence

Leverage process flows and decisions to route requests to the appropriate department or system.

### Approve or Reroute

Design loops with human feedback and ask Azure OpenAI to improve its decisions.
