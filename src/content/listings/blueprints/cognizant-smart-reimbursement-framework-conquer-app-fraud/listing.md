Cognizant's Smart APP-Fraud Reimbursement Framework, powered by the Camunda platform, offers a comprehensive solution, addressing the limitations of Reimbursement Claims Management System (RCMS) and empowering Payment Systems Regulators (PSRs) to effectively combat Authorized Push Payment (APP) fraud.

## Features

### Seamless RCMS Integration

Automates claim creation, reducing manual effort and ensuring data consistency between your systems and RCMS.

### Enhanced System Integration

Connects with your CRM, Fraud Prevention, and Customer Communication platforms for a holistic view of customer interactions and fraud events.

### Customizable Workflows

Tailor the system to your specific needs, implementing business rules, SLAs, and automated processes.

### Advanced Fraud Insights

Leverage data analytics to identify trends, patterns, and vulnerabilities, enabling proactive fraud prevention.

### Intuitive User Interface

Simplify complex tasks for fraud specialists with user-friendly dashboards and streamlined workflows.
