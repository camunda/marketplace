*Aaseya ALPS: One-Stop Licensing for Businesses and Residents*

Streamline government licensing for residents and businesses. Manage applications, payments, and inspections to boost efficiency and revenue.

## Features

### Hunter/User License Creation

Manage the end-to-end licensing process for hunters, recreational users, and other individuals requiring a specific type of license.

### Hunter/User Review

Enable efficient review and processing of license applications, ensuring that applications are thoroughly checked and compliant with regulatory standards before approval.

### Hunter/User License Creation Approval

Streamline the final stages of the licensing process, ensuring applications meet all regulatory requirements before official issuance.

### Document Verification

Ensure applicants meet eligibility requirements by verifying submitted documents accurately and efficiently.

### Checklist Evaluation

Streamline and ensure the accuracy of eligibility assessment, document verification, and regulatory compliance for each license application.
