*Revolutionizing Data Consistency in Telecom with Camunda*

Centralize customer information by integrating CRM tools and legacy systems to create a single source of truth. Ensure data consistency, minimize discrepancies, and keep customer records accurate and up-to-date for enhanced efficiency and reliability.

## Features

### Enhanced Data Quality

By consolidating data and automating validation checks, the telecom provider can significantly reduce issues such as duplicate, outdated, and incorrect information. The centralized repository now acts as a dependable and accurate source for customer data, enhancing all downstream processes that depend on this information—resulting in smoother operations and a better overall customer experience.

### Centralized Data Repository

Consolidates customer data from CRM tools, legacy systems, and third-party platforms into a single, unified repository. This eliminates silos and ensures all systems access the same reliable and consistent data.

### Real-Time Data Synchronization

Utilizes APIs to enable real-time synchronization of data across systems. This ensures that any updates to customer information are immediately reflected across all integrated platforms.

### Automated Data Validation

Implements validation rules within the business process to detect inconsistencies such as missing fields, duplicate entries, and incorrect formats. These rules ensure data quality and reduce manual correction efforts.

### Discrepancy Resolution Workflow

Automatically assigns flagged inconsistencies to the appropriate teams or systems for resolution. This minimizes delays and ensures errors are corrected at the source with minimal manual intervention.
