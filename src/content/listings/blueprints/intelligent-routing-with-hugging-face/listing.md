*Enhanced Process Efficiency with Hugging Face AI*

Transform Camunda-based business processes and operationalize AI/ML with the Hugging Face Connector and process blueprint.

## Features

### Contextualizing Signals

Process customer signals with Hugging Face models and business-context prompts.

### Task Intelligence

Leverage process flow and decisions to route requests to the appropriate department or system.

### Approve or Reroute

Design loops with human feedback and ask Hugging Face to improve its decisions.
