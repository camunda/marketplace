Use BPMN to orchestrate processes that enforce SLAs.

## Features

### SLA Monitoring and Alerts

Implements continuous monitoring of process execution times against SLAs, generating alerts for potential breaches.

### Automated SLA Compliance Actions

Defines automated actions to be taken when potential SLA breaches are detected, minimizing delays.
