Easily connect Camunda to HubSpot to manage contacts, companies, deals, and forms. Automate CRM tasks such as creating and updating records, searching data, and enrolling contacts in workflowsall without writing code.

## Features

### Manage contacts

Retrieve all contacts, get contacts by ID, or search for contacts.You can also create, update, and delete contacts.

### Manage companies

Retrieve all companies, get company by ID, or search for companies. You can also create and delete companies.

### Manage companies' contacts

Get all contacts of a company, add contacts to a company, or remove contacts from a company.

### Manage deals

Retrieve all deals, get deal by ID, or search for deals. You can also use this feature to delete deals.

### Additional features

Submit forms, enroll contacts in a workflow, and add elements to lists.
