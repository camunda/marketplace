Accelerates approval, automate compliance checks, and ensure clean master data. Achieve faster vendor creation, reduce risk, and unify ERP visibility with intelligent AI orchestration.

## Features

### AI-driven document processing

Extracts and validates supplier documents like tax forms, banking details, and certifications, reducing manual effort and ensuring data accuracy.

### Automated risk and compliance checks

AI and DMN rules classify suppliers by risk level, check sanction lists, and flag license expiries, ensuring faster, compliant onboarding.

### Duplicate supplier detection

Prevents data duplication across ERP and CRM systems, maintaining a single source of truth and cleaner master data.

### Smart exception management

Routes flagged cases to compliance or finance automatically, while AI drafts supplier communication emails, minimizing back-and-forth delays.

### Unified ERP creation and visibility

Seamlessly integrates validated suppliers into ERP, synchronizing data and enabling complete visibility across procurement systems.
