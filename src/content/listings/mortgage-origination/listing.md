This blueprint demonstrates how to improve the mortgage origination process by automating validation and compliance tasks. It focuses on development skills, best practices, and architectural understanding.

## Features

### Buyer Validation

Validate important information about the buyer with tasks such as document verification, credit check, income verification, and so on.

### Property Validation

Validate important information about the property being purchased with tasks such as title insurance validation, property appraisal, insurance (proof of insurability), condo or homeowners' association (HOA) checks, and so on.

### Underwriting

Integrate mortgage underwriting that's executed externally (with data integrations) and internally.

### Approval Cycle

Incorporate a review cycle that includes notifications with regulatory recording.

### Settlement and Funding

Orchestrate settlement and funding activities with the required bank information, wire details, and so on.
