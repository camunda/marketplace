Easily retrieve or search for process instances, process definitions, variables, flow node Instances, and incidents. Manage and monitor all of these within your Camunda workflow.

## Features

### Retrieve or Search Process Instances

Obtain specific process instances for auditing, analytics, or process optimization.

### Access Process Definitions

Fetch definitions to understand the architecture and flow of deployed processes.

### Manage Variables

Get or search for variables to understand data flows within your processes.

### Examine Flow Node Instances

Study specific nodes in your process flows for diagnostics and improvement.

### Handle Incidents

Retrieve incidents for troubleshooting and root cause analysis.
