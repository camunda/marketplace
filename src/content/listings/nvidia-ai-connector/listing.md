Integrate multiple AI models into Camunda-based business processes with dynamic prompts, structured responses, and cost-effective automation using free tiers and trial credits.

## Features

### AI-driven process execution

Execute AI-powered tasks within business processes by sending prompts and receiving responses in real time, enhancing automation and decision-making.

### Multi-model AI integration

Invoke multiple NVIDIA AI models dynamically within Camunda-based processes by simply configuring the model name and prompt, enabling flexible AI-driven process automation.

### Configurable and cost-effective usage

Supports configurable inputs, structured responses, and access to free models and trial credits, enabling efficient and cost-effective AI experimentation within business processes.
