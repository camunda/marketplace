*Seamless Migration to Camunda 8 — Structured, Secure, Predictable.*

Seamlessly migrate from Camunda 7 to Camunda 8 with Integrity Vision. We deliver secure, low-risk transitions with expert support, architecture alignment, API adaptation, and historical data migration — tailored for your business needs.

## Features

### Comprehensive Camunda 7 Assessment & Migration Roadmap

We begin with a comprehensive audit of your Camunda 7 environment, encompassing processes, custom code, and integrations, to define a migration scope tailored to your technical and business requirements. Our team delivers a realistic roadmap with timelines, risk analysis, and a delivery model aligned with your goals.

### Camunda 8 Architecture Design & Hardening

We design a modern, scalable Camunda 8 architecture (Kubernetes or VM-based), tailored to your security, availability, and compliance needs. This includes system hardening, observability, and disaster recovery setup — all built with financial-grade robustness in mind.

### End-to-End Migration of Processes, Logic & APIs

From BPMN models to execution listeners, external clients, and custom APIs — we transform your Camunda 7 logic into Camunda 8-ready components using job-workers, FEEL, and secure, maintainable integration patterns. Tasklists and UI apps are also upgraded to match the new engine.

### Historical Data Migration & BI Readiness

We help you securely migrate historical data from Camunda 7 databases into centralized or external stores. We also reconfigure reporting tools and BI platforms to support analytics from Camunda 8 datasets, ensuring compliance, traceability, and continuity of insights.

### CI/CD Enablement & Delivery Automation

To support modern operations, we introduce DevOps practices for Camunda 8: BPMN validation, automated testing, and end-to-end deployment pipelines — enabling fast, safe, and repeatable delivery of process updates and applications.
