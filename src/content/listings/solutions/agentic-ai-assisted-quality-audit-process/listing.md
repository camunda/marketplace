*Agentic AI Assisted Quality Audit Process*

Reduce audit time from 138 to 8 minutes, cutting costs, improving agent performance, and transforming customer experience for call centers.

## Features

### Complete call understanding with natural language processing (NLP)

Unlike keyword spotting, the AI understands context, tone, intent, and sentiment.

### Drastically reduce audit time

Reduce overall audit cycle time, enabling 100% call coverage instead of random samples, with massive cost savings and more effective audits.

### Automated quality scoring and compliance checks

Score calls against customizable criteria including script adherence, regulatory compliance, upsell attempts, and so on. Ensure consistency across auditors and reduce the risk of human error or bias.

### Get real-time insights into trends

Identify patterns across thousands of calls, including recurring issues, agent performance trends, and missed sales opportunities. This feature empower teams to focus on continuous improvement and proactive decision-making.

### Guided coaching recommendations

Generates actionable feedback for agents such as “improve product knowledge” and "missed upsell on plan upgrade.” This accelerates training and improves agent performance at scale.
