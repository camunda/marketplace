The Compliance Monitoring Agent ensures consistent oversight and better audit readiness for events in highly regulated industries by automating risk detection and safeguards without human intervention.

## Features

### Automated monitoring and analysis

Instantly review every recorded event with AI, eliminating manual effort and ensuring continuous oversight.

### Consistent and real-time policy detection

Identify policy violations immediately with AI, replacing subjective human judgment with objective assessments.

### Context-aware role validation

Ensure sensitive data is only discussed by authorized roles, safeguarding against unauthorized disclosures.

### Scalable compliance audits

Achieve full coverage of all events without adding headcount, ensuring compliance at scale.

### Traceable AI reasoning and continuous improvement

Provides reliable compliance trails with explainable logic, enhancing audit readiness.
AI continuously learns from real-world cases, increasing accuracy and reducing false positives over time.
