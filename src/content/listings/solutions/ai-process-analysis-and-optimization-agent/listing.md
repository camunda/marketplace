*Continuously Analyze and Optimize Processes with AI*

An AI agent continuously analyzes process performance and orchestrates optimization actions with governance and human oversight.

## Features

### Continuous process analysis

AI monitors execution data and evaluates performance indicators in real time.

### Bottleneck and anomaly detection

Identifies delays, recurring incidents, and performance deviations.

### AI-generated recommendations

Produces actionable insights to improve business processes and resource allocation.

### Closed-loop orchestration

Triggers improvement workflows directly in Camunda.

### Governed human oversight

Supports advisory and semi-autonomous modes with full auditability.
