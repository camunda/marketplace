*Accelerate motor claim decisions with AI*

Orchestrate motor insurance claims in Camunda with AI-assisted validation, document extraction, human approvals, and event-driven milestones from intake to payout.

## Features

### Orchestrate claims from start to finish

Coordinate the full motor insurance claim lifecycle in a single journey, from claim intake and policy validation through document checks, estimate review, repair governance, and settlement closure.

### Validation assisted by IDP and AI

Combine IDP-based document extraction with AI-assisted risk and pricing analysis to improve validation accuracy, reduce repetitive manual checks, and provide better decision support at critical review stages.

### Enforce role-based human approvals

Add human-in-the-loop checkpoints for surveyor and product expert reviews, with clear escalation, rework, resubmission, and rejection paths to support consistent, accountable decisions.

### Advanced milestones with messages

Use correlated message events to trigger real-world milestones such as survey image submission and estimate upload, enabling asynchronous progress without blocking upstream execution.

### Handle exceptions with bounded retries

Apply bounded retries and defined fallback paths for validation and approval failures to prevent infinite loops and ensure every claim reaches a clear next action or terminal outcome.
