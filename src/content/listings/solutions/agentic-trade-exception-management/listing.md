Streamline and automate trade exception processes, reducing the cost of operational risks and increasing regulatory compliance in financial services institutions.

### Simplify processes and reduce risk

Use automation to reduce human errors by up to 100%.

### Shorten settlement cycles and increase efficiency

Achieve 45% lower exception handling time and 35% reduction in exception and error handling cost.

### Process automation across the trade lifecycle

Camunda orchestrates the entire trade lifecycle.

### Improve regulatory compliance and achieve faster settlements

End-to-end agentic orchestration drives faster settlements and lowers compliance risk.

### Future-ready trade lifecycle automation

A model-agnostic framework allows for plug-and-play upgrades to the model.
