Upgrade your Camunda workflows with integrated WhatsApp messaging. The WhatsApp Connector enables automated, secure, and efficient communications directly from your business processes.

## Features

### Send Message

Whether you're sending notifications to customers about their orders, alerting team members about process changes, or providing automated responses for FAQs, the ability to send messages via WhatsApp directly from Camunda processes simplifies communication. Automate confirmations, alerts, and updates without leaving Camunda.
