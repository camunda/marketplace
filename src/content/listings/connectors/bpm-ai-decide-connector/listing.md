*Automate decision-making in business processes to reduce reliance on human tasks and improve operational performance*

Make automated decisions in business processes based on process variables and a question.

## Features

### Automated Decisions and Classification

Supercharge your process automation with AI decision-making capabilities. Designed for versatility, the Connector handles general decision tasks, document classification, and more, streamlining your workflow and improving operational efficiency.

### Explainability and Confidence Estimation

Elevate trust in automated decisions with explainability features that provide reasoning descriptions for decisions that are made by AI models. Set a confidence threshold to automatically defer to human judgment when the model is uncertain, ensuring high accuracy and oversight in decision-making.

### Multimodal Support

Accept input data in various forms and modalities including PDFs, images and audio. You can input documents via file paths or web URLs, making the Connector adaptable to a wide range of sources and ensuring seamless integration into your existing processes.

### Integrated OCR Capabilities

Extract text from images and scanned documents with embedded or API-based OCR, enabling decisions based on non-text documents, even if the model only supports text.

### On-premises Support

Leverage built-in support for local AI models for basic use cases, ensuring data privacy, cost efficiency, and enhanced reliability within your infrastructure.
