*Automate personalized emails in business processes*

Leverage the power of Thymeleaf and the Spring Framework to send personalized and localized emails.

## Features

### Send Localized Emails

Send emails in the recipient's language.

### Inject Variable Data

Variables that you specify are passed to the Thymeleaf email template and can be injected into the email. This feature enables you to create personalized emails with proper greetings, including salutation and name. Variables can also be injected into the subject as message arguments.
