*Connect Camunda processes to Valkey*

Connect Camunda processes with Valkey for caching, key-value storage, collections, counters, expiration, and Pub/Sub messaging. Connect to standalone and cluster deployments with authentication and SSL/TLS.

## Features

### Run a wide range of Valkey operations

Perform key-value, hash, list, set, counter, key-management, expiration, and pub/sub operations directly from Camunda processes.

### Connect to standalone and cluster deployments

Connect securely to Redis or Valkey in standalone or cluster environments with authentication and SSL/TLS support.

### Enable event-driven messaging with pub/sub

Publish and subscribe to messages through Redis or Valkey channels, enabling event-driven communication within Camunda processes.
