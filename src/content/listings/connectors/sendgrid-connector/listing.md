*Streamline Email Communications in Camunda-Based Processes*

Integrate SendGrid email delivery into your Camunda processes for automated notifications and reliable transactional emails.

## Features

### Send Message

Leverage Camunda to send customized emails via SendGrid. This feature is essential for a wide range of activities, including customer notifications, transactional emails, and internal communications within a business process.

### Send Message Using Template

Leverage SendGrid's templating feature to send standardized, yet personalized, emails right from within your Camunda processes. This feature is ideal for automated marketing campaigns, customer onboarding, and regular status updates.
