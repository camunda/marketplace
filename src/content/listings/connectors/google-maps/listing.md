*Seamlessly Integrate Geographic Services with Camunda and Google Maps*

Bring the power of Google Maps into your Camunda workflows. Camunda's Google Maps Connector automates address validation, place identification, and distance calculations, enabling streamlined operations across sectors such as e-commerce, logistics, and customer services

## Features

### Validate Address

Automatically validate customer addresses during form submissions, sign-up processes, or other data collection stages.

### Get Place ID

Retrieve Google's unique place identifier for locations such as businesses, landmarks, or geographical points of interest, useful for enhancing business analytics or recommendation systems.

### Calculate Distance

Automate the calculation of distances between locations for delivery routes, cost estimations, or logistics planning.
