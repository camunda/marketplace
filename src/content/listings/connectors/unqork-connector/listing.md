*Combine Camunda process orchestration with Unqork's no-code development capabilities*

Integrate Camunda-based business processes with Unqork's no-code application development capabilities to deliver business value quickly.

## Features

### Initiate Bi-directional Processes

Start an Unqork-based process from Camunda or trigger a Camunda-based process from an Unqork application.

### Create Custom Schemas

Tailor the data output of both Camunda- and Unqork-based processes to fit your specific use case.

### Connect Your Ecosystem Seamlessly

Integrate the combination of Camunda and Unqork into both upstream and downstream applications and processes.
