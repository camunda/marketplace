## Features

### Standardized OSPlus access

Provides standardized access to selected OSPlus services for Camunda-based business processes.

### Secure banking integration

Designed for regulated Sparkassen environments with controlled data access.

### End-to-end orchestration

Enables orchestration of banking processes beyond standard OSPlus automation.
