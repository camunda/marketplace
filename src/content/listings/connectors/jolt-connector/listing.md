*Perform JSON-to-JSON transformations with ease*

Make complex data mapping tasks more accessible and efficient for process automation.

## Features

### Accelerated development and time-to-value

Significantly reduces the time and effort required to integrate Camunda processes with diverse external systems, especially when complex data transformations are involved.

### Enhanced data transformation capabilities

Perform complex JSON-to-JSON transformations, adapting data structures to meet the requirements of different systems without custom code.

### Reusability drives efficiency

Reusing existing connector logic and templates reduces redundant development efforts and associated costs.
