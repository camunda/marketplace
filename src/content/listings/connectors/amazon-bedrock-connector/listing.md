Integrate any AI model that Amazon Bedrock offers into your Camunda-based business processes.

## Features

### Invoke Model

Make a custom request to any model that is available on Amazon Bedrock.

### Converse

Bring a conversational agent into your business processes, using any model available on Amazon Bedrock.
