Connect Camunda with Automic Automation to start jobs, get metadata, and route process paths based on execution status for reliable automation across systems.

## Features

### Orchestrate cross-system automation

Combine Camunda process modeling and decision logic with Automic execution and scheduling to automate complex, cross-system processes from start to finish.

### Trigger Automic jobs from Camunda

Start Automic workflows and jobs directly from Camunda service tasks to orchestrate technical automation as part of end-to-end business processes.

### React to job outcomes in-process

Retrieve execution status and outcomes from Automic to drive gateways, retries, and escalation paths in Camunda so processes respond correctly to success or failure.
