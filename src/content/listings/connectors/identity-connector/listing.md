*Manage users, roles, groups, and clients from a process*

Use more than 20 functions to create, search, and delete users, roles, and groups, and to create and delete clients. You can also create authorizations and manage items, such as adding users to groups, so all identity operations are automated as part of a process.

## Features

### Manage users

Create, search, and delete users.

### Manage roles

Create, search, and delete roles.

### Manage groups

Create, search, and delete groups.

### Manage clients

Create and delete clients.
