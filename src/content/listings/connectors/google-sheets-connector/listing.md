Integrate Camunda with Google Sheets for automated, real-time data management. Ideal for data collection, reporting, and dynamic data structuring within orchestrated processes.

## Features

### Create Spreadsheet

Automatically generate a new Google Sheet from a Camunda process, serving as a dynamic data repository for various process steps, such as reporting or data analysis.

### Add Value to Spreadsheet

Populate your Google Sheet with data right from within Camunda, be it for tracking, data collection, or dashboard updates.

### Get Spreadsheet Details

Retrieve metadata and other relevant details of Google Sheets for validation or to inform subsequent steps in your Camunda process.

### Create Worksheet

Create additional worksheets within an existing Google Sheet to organize data in a more segmented manner, all without leaving your Camunda process.

### Get Worksheet Data

Pull data from specific worksheets for processing or analysis within Camunda, automating data-driven decisions and actions.

### Delete Worksheet

Remove unnecessary or outdated worksheets from Google Sheets, aiding in data maintenance and organization within Camunda.

### Create Row

Add new rows to a Google Sheet for data appending needs, be it for log tracking or inventory management.

### Create Empty Column and Row

Prepare your Google Sheet for future data by adding empty columns and rows, providing a scalable structure for dynamic data entry.

### Delete Column

Remove irrelevant columns to keep your Google Sheets data clean and focused, facilitating easier data management in Camunda.

### Get Row by Index

Retrieve specific rows based on their index for targeted data manipulation or reporting within Camunda workflows.
