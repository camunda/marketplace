Integrate Camunda with OutSystems for real-time data exchange, efficient case management, and streamlined business operations.

## Features

### Automatically Create Inspection Cases

Trigger the creation of inspection cases in OutSystems directly from Camunda-based processes. Automate and streamline the case initiation process, reducing manual intervention and ensuring timely execution

### Seamless Data Exchange

Enable real-time data synchronization between Camunda and OutSystems. Ensure accurate and consistent information flow, minimizing errors and improving process transparency.

### Orchestrate Processes with BPMN

Leverage Camunda’s BPMN capabilities to design and orchestrate complex processes while delegating inspection case management to OutSystems, ensuring robust process execution.

### Enhanced Case Management in OutSystems

Use OutSystems to manage the entire lifecycle of inspection cases. Access detailed case updates, handle custom logic, and monitor progress seamlessly.

### Scalable and Extensible Solution

Built to handle high volumes of inspection cases, the integration is scalable and can be extended to accommodate additional processes and business domains.
