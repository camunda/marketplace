Connect Camunda processes to Google Cloud Storage to upload and download files easily. Ideal for cloud-based document management and automation scenarios.

## Features

### Upload files

Upload files to Google Cloud Storage buckets directly from Camunda-based business processes.

### Download files

Download files from Google Cloud Storage buckets into the Camunda process context for further processing, external delivery, or archiving.
