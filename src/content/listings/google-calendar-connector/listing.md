Create, update, and delete Google Calendar events directly from your Camunda process, including recurring events and individual occurrences.

## Features

### Create Google Calendar events

Create a Google Calendar event and set its time zone, color, title, description, or recurrence.

### Update events and recurring occurrences

Update any attribute on a Google Calendar event, or update one or more occurrences of a recurring event.

### Delete events and recurring occurrences

Delete a single event, including a recurring event, or delete specific occurrences of a recurring event.

### Retrieve and search events

Get a specific event, or search for events within a time period.
