Use SSH to securely connect to remote servers and execute commands on them as part of your Camunda-based business processes.

## Features

### Execute Command

Automate the execution of shell commands on remote servers using SSH directly from your Camunda processes. Whether you're managing deployments, triggering scripts, or performing system maintenance, this feature allows you to securely and efficiently interact with your servers without manual intervention.

This feature enables you to seamlessly incorporate server-side operations into your automated workflows, offering a unified approach to system management and process automation.
