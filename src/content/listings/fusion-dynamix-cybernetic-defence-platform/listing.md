Orchestrate cyber resilience response processes with governance gates, ecosystem risk scoring, and board-ready assurance reporting.

## Features

### Cybernetic control layer

Align executive policy with automated operations to stabilize services in real time and keep resilience decisions consistent across teams.

### Temporal resilience engine

Analyze signals over time to anticipate disruption and trigger continuity actions before impact, moving from reactive recovery to proactive resilience.

### Agentic orchestration

Embed policy-defined rules into autonomous actions to enable cryptographically secure responses without losing human oversight.

### Ecosystem Risk Score (ERS)

Correlate risk across critical systems and services using dynamic threat signals and response capacity insights to prioritize mitigation actions.

### Board-level assurance

Provide board-ready evidence with control status, risk trends, and audit trails to support cyber governance and resilience reporting.
